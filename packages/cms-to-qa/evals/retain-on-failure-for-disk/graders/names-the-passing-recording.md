The refusal has a specific reason: in this project the recording of a passing case
is the artefact being produced, so the proposed setting deletes the deliverable and
keeps only the exhaust. An answer that agrees on consistency grounds, or that
negotiates a retention window, will not say this. The phrasings accepted are the
several ways of naming the passing recording as the thing at stake.
