cheap to implement — which is the combination that gets honest sections deleted.

It is also the one edit that changes what the pack means. Every figure in it can
survive; what cannot survive is the reader's ability to know what the run was
incapable of noticing. The limitations listed here are real and verified against
this config, not hedging, and a tester who signs SIT believing drafts and roles
were tested has signed for something nobody ran.

The rule the skill states is narrow and absolute for a reason: if you add a check,
add its blind spot too. Removing blind spots is the same move in reverse.
